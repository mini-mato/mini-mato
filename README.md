### Tim Miano

Founder & CEO, [Proffer Futures](https://github.com/proffer-futures) — building [**ifwishlist**](https://ifwishlist.com), a conditional-commitment platform for collective action.

**I will if enough others will.**

Conditional commitment turns latent willingness — "I would, if enough others would" — into coordinated action: pledges that activate only once enough of the right people opt in. I work at the seam between AI and the institutions that govern collective action — a lawyer and former tax attorney by training, now designing and building AI-mediated coordination systems that run inside explicit legal and privacy constraints.

**Building** — [ifwishlist.com](https://ifwishlist.com): an AI-built coordination platform with a no-custody money architecture and a _k_-anonymity privacy floor enforced in code.

**Research** — [Clearing Conditional Commitments](https://github.com/mini-matters/clearing-conditional-commitments): a working paper on the mechanism design of a conditional-commitment clearinghouse — custody, specification timing, and disclosure (_k_-anonymity) as the three design dials. _JEL C72, D82, D47, H41._

**Also** — [linelight](https://github.com/mini-matters/linelight), a scientifically-honest atomic-physics instrument suite for the browser; [holodeck](https://github.com/mini-matters/holodeck), a single-file WebGL toy.
